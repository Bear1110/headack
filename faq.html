常見問題

Headack 是什麼？

免費的頭痛日記網站。頭痛時點一下就能記錄開始時間，再用幾個大按鈕補上痛的程度、位置、症狀和用藥；平常可以看日曆、每月統計，看診時列印摘要給醫師。紀錄存在你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。

為什麼叫 Headack？

Headack 是 headache（頭痛）加上 ack（acknowledge，確認收到）。頭痛如果沒有被好好「ack」——被正視、被記下來——就很難看清楚原因，也很難和醫師一起找到有效的處理方式。所以我們希望大家多記錄自己的頭痛和用藥。

我的資料存在哪裡？網站作者看得到嗎？

登入後，紀錄存在你自己 Google 雲端硬碟裡的一份試算表（「Headack 頭痛紀錄」）；尚未同步的紀錄暫存在你目前使用的瀏覽器。本網站沒有後端伺服器，作者看不到任何人的資料。詳見隱私權政策。

一定要登入嗎？

不用登入也能記錄，方便頭痛當下馬上使用。但沒登入時，紀錄只存在這台裝置的瀏覽器裡：清除瀏覽器資料、換手機或換瀏覽器，紀錄就會不見。建議用 Google 登入，紀錄會備份到你自己的雲端硬碟。

網站會看到我雲端硬碟裡的其他檔案嗎？

不會。登入時授權的是 Google 的 drive.file 權限，只能存取本網站自己建立的那份試算表。

換手機或換電腦怎麼辦？

在新裝置打開網站，用同一個 Google 帳號登入，紀錄就會從你的試算表讀回來。

沒有網路的時候可以記錄嗎？

可以。紀錄會先存在瀏覽器裡，連上網路後自動同步到試算表。

怎麼加到手機主畫面？

按畫面上方的「↓」按鈕。Android 和電腦版 Chrome／Edge 會直接安裝；iPhone 請在 Safari 點「分享」→「加入主畫面」。

怎麼把紀錄給醫師看？

到「統計」→「看診摘要」，選好期間後按「列印／存成 PDF」。摘要包含每月頭痛天數、用藥天數、發作特徵與治療反應。也可以直接分享你的 Google 試算表。

用藥過度的提醒是怎麼算的？

依國際頭痛分類（ICHD-3）的參考門檻：一般止痛藥每月使用 15 天以上，翠普登類、複方止痛藥、麥角類或鴉片類每月 10 天以上。達到時會提醒你和醫師討論，因為止痛藥用太頻繁本身也可能造成頭痛。這只是提醒，不是診斷。

怎麼匯入以前的紀錄？

到「設定」→「匯入紀錄」。可以把你在筆記或其他 App 的紀錄交給 AI（ChatGPT、Claude 等）整理成格式後貼回來，或直接選擇本網站匯出的 JSON 檔。本網站不會把你的資料傳給 AI，資料是你自己貼過去的。

天氣功能會追蹤我的位置嗎？

天氣是選用功能，預設關閉。開啟後只會把大約位置（約 10 公里範圍）傳給 Open-Meteo 查詢氣壓、氣溫與濕度，不傳送任何健康資料，試算表裡也不存位置。

怎麼刪除所有資料？

到「設定」→「危險區域」→「清空所有紀錄」。也可以直接刪除雲端硬碟中的試算表，並在 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權頁面撤銷本網站的授權。

這是醫療器材嗎？

不是。本網站只是記錄工具，統計與提醒不構成醫療診斷。如果出現突然的劇烈頭痛、單側無力、說話困難等症狀，請立即就醫。

有問題或建議要找誰？

歡迎到 GitHub 提出 issue，也可以在那裡協助翻譯。

FAQ

What is Headack?

A free headache diary. Log the start of a headache with one tap, add intensity, location, symptoms and medication with big buttons, see a calendar and monthly stats, and print a summary for your doctor. Your entries are stored in your own Google Drive.

Why “Headack”?

Headack = headache + ack (acknowledge). A headache that isn't acked — noticed and written down — is hard to understand and hard to treat well with your doctor. So we hope you'll log your headaches and medication.

Where is my data? Can the author see it?

Once you sign in, entries are stored in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive; entries not yet synced are kept in your browser. There is no backend server — the author cannot see anyone's data. See the privacy policy.

Do I have to sign in?

No — you can log right away. But without signing in, entries live only in this browser on this device: clearing browser data or switching phones or browsers will lose them. Sign in with Google to back them up to your own Drive.

Can the site see other files in my Drive?

No. It uses Google's drive.file permission, which only allows access to the spreadsheet the site created.

What if I switch phones or computers?

Open the site on the new device and sign in with the same Google account; your entries are read back from your spreadsheet.

Can I log entries offline?

Yes. Entries are saved in the browser first and synced to your spreadsheet when you're back online.

How do I add it to my home screen?

Tap the “↓” button at the top. Android and desktop Chrome/Edge install it directly; on iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari.

How do I show my records to a doctor?

Go to Stats → Doctor summary, choose a period and tap “Print / save as PDF”. You can also share your Google spreadsheet.

How is the medication-overuse reminder calculated?

It uses the ICHD-3 reference thresholds: simple analgesics on 15 or more days a month; triptans, combination analgesics, ergots or opioids on 10 or more days a month. It's a reminder to talk with your doctor, not a diagnosis.

How do I import older records?

Settings → Import entries. Let an AI (ChatGPT, Claude, …) convert your notes using the provided prompt and paste the reply back, or choose a JSON file exported from this site. The site never sends your data to any AI — you paste it yourself.

Does the weather feature track my location?

Weather is optional and off by default. When on, only your approximate location (about 10 km) is sent to Open-Meteo to look up pressure, temperature and humidity. No health data is sent and your location is not stored.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Danger zone → Delete all entries. You can also delete the spreadsheet from your Drive and revoke the site's access on your Google Account.

Is this a medical device?

No. It's a record-keeping tool; statistics and alerts are not a diagnosis. Seek medical care immediately for a sudden severe headache, one-sided weakness or difficulty speaking.
