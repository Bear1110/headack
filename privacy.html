隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-04

我們不收集你的資料

Headack（以下稱「本網站」）是放在 GitHub Pages 上的靜態網頁，沒有後端伺服器。你的頭痛紀錄只會存放在兩個地方：

  • 你自己 Google 雲端硬碟中的一份 Google 試算表（登入後由本網站建立）；
  • 你目前使用的瀏覽器的本機儲存空間（用於離線記錄與尚未同步的資料）。

網站作者無法存取上述任何資料。

Google 帳號權限

  • drive.file：只能存取本網站建立的檔案，或你親自選取的檔案；看不到你雲端硬碟中的其他內容。
  • openid、email：只用來在畫面上顯示你目前登入的帳號。

本網站對 Google 使用者資料的使用遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 規定。資料不會傳送給任何第三方，也不會用於廣告。

天氣記錄（選用）

你可以在「設定」開啟自動記錄天氣。開啟後，新增頭痛時瀏覽器會向 Open-Meteo 查詢當地的氣壓、氣溫與濕度。送出的只有四捨五入到約 10 公里範圍的座標，不包含任何健康資料；位置不會存進你的試算表。這項功能預設關閉，隨時可以關掉。

無廣告、無追蹤 Cookie

本網站不放廣告，不使用 Google Analytics 這類追蹤工具，也不設定任何 Cookie。

匿名瀏覽量統計

為了知道網站有多少人使用，每個頁面會向 GoatCounter 載入一張 1×1 的計數圖片。這只會記錄「哪一頁被瀏覽了一次」，以及瀏覽器一般會送出的資訊（例如瀏覽器類型、大約的國家）。它不執行任何程式碼、不設 Cookie、不追蹤個人，也完全接觸不到你的頭痛紀錄或 Google 帳號。

AI 分析與匯入（選用）

使用「AI 分析」時，你選擇的紀錄會由你的瀏覽器帶到你選擇的 AI 服務（例如 ChatGPT、Claude），或複製到剪貼簿由你自行貼上。本網站不會傳送、也看不到這些內容；該服務如何處理資料，由它自己的隱私權政策規範。使用「匯入紀錄」時，是你自己把資料貼到 AI，本網站提供的提示詞只有格式說明。

刪除資料

你可以隨時直接刪除雲端硬碟中的試算表；在本網站「設定」中登出會清除瀏覽器內的本機資料。你也可以在 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權 頁面撤銷本網站的授權。

非醫療器材

本網站僅供個人記錄，統計與提醒不構成醫療診斷或建議。

政策變更

本政策如有修改，會更新本頁的「最後更新」日期。

聯絡

有任何問題，請到 GitHub 提出 issue。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

We do not collect your data

Headack ("the site") is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages with no backend server. Your headache entries are stored only in:

  • a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your own Google Drive, created by this site after you sign in; and
  • your browser's local storage, for offline entries and data not yet synced.

The site author cannot access any of this data.

Google account permissions

  • drive.file: access only to files this site created or files you explicitly select. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • openid, email: used only to show which account you are signed in with.

This site's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is never sent to third parties or used for advertising.

Weather recording (optional)

You can turn on automatic weather recording in Settings. When it is on and you log a headache, your browser asks Open-Meteo for the local air pressure, temperature and humidity. Only coordinates rounded to about 10 km are sent — no health data — and your location is not saved to your spreadsheet. It is off by default and can be turned off at any time.

No ads, no tracking cookies

The site shows no ads, uses no tracking tools such as Google Analytics, and sets no cookies.

Anonymous page-view counts

To know how many people use the site, each page loads a 1×1 counter image from GoatCounter. It records only that a page was viewed, plus what browsers normally send (such as browser type and approximate country). It runs no code, sets no cookies, does not track individuals, and has no access to your headache entries or Google account.

AI analysis and import (optional)

When you use AI analysis, the entries you choose are taken by your browser to the AI service you pick (such as ChatGPT or Claude), or copied to your clipboard for you to paste. This site does not send or see that content; the service's own privacy policy applies. When you import, you paste your own data into an AI; the prompt provided by this site contains only format instructions.

Deleting your data

You can delete the spreadsheet from your Drive at any time. Signing out in Settings clears the data stored in this browser. You can also revoke this site's access on your Google Account third-party access page.

Not a medical device

This site is for personal record-keeping only. Statistics and alerts are not medical diagnosis or advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date on this page will be updated.

Contact

For any questions, please open an issue on GitHub.
